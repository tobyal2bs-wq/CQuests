# CQuests 1.14.1

A small update that goes with CBosses 1.1.0.

## Changes

- Boss parts stay drawn when you look up at a big boss from close by (each part gets a display box the size of the boss, so the client no longer culls it).
- Boss models can bring their own animations.
- World bosses still step aside when CBosses is installed (`world-bosses.defer-to-cbosses: true`). CBosses 1.1.0 now runs the weekly bosses, 30 hourly mini bosses, the Gourd King and the decor pumpkins; CQuests keeps its quest chapter bosses and boss spots.

## Upgrading

Replace the jar and restart. No config changes are needed.
