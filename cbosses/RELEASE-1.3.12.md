# CQuests 1.17.2 and CBosses 1.3.12: every model checked for loose parts

Every one of the 101 models was played through all of its animations and checked for parts that come away from the body.

## Joined
- 77 loose parts on 41 models were found. Torsos that sat apart from the hips (Slagheart, the Crimson Ronin, Captain Brine, Killer Claus, Old Man Winter, Brakka, Overgrowth), arms that left the shoulder in a swing, heads, tails and fangs are now joined with a joint that holds through every move.
- Held things are in the hand now: the four Hollows' staffs, King Oldbeard's staff, the Mayor's gavel, the Grave Marshal's shield and the Old Wizard's book.
- Parts that float on purpose (halos, rings, lures) are left alone.

## Flicker
- About 25,000 surfaces that shared a plane with another one were moved 0.03 of a pixel apart on all models. That is what made blocks flicker and glitch on older bosses.

## Colour patches
- 1,632 random light and dark square patches were taken off the older models. They read as blocks of a colour that had nothing to do with the model. Eyes, mouths, stripes and buckles are kept.
