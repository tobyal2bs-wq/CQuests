# CBosses 1.3.3

## Changes

- Removed: Jellyboo, Borka the Bruiser, Gulp the Cyclops, the Cinder Devil, the Boo Twins, the Shulker Matriarch and the Pink Burrower. Servers drop them from bosses.yml on start. Chapters 6 and 8 have no boss now; ops can put any boss there with /cbosses chapter set.
- Redrawn in clean colours, with real legs and arms: Hay Golem, Moss Imp, Gilded Mimic, Mossling, Mister Pebbles, Snapjaw and Stumpy.
- The Rotting Giant's arms now hang almost to the ground and end in plain fists (no fingers). He and the Grave Marshal now climb out of the ground in three heaves, with dirt bursting up each time.
- New settings page for ops, "Evolving and mythic bosses" (/cbosses settings evolve): evolve chance, timing, stages and power, plus mythic health and damage. On 1.21.6+ it opens in the game's own settings screen.
- The README now matches the current boss list and the two-legend finale.
