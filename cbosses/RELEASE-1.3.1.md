# CBosses 1.3.1

## Changes

- The mini bosses are back to their earlier looks (Hay Golem, Snapjaw, Snik, Mister Pebbles, The Pink Burrower, Moss Imp, Mossling, Bark Ape, Old Hoodlum and the rest). The 1.3.0 redraws were too busy. They keep the 1.3.0 sizes and centring.
- The new big bosses use clean colours instead of patchy textures.
- Sir Clucks-a-Lot has been removed. Servers that still have him drop him from bosses.yml on start.
- The twins and brothers are one model again: Hoo, Thimble and Puffball are gone.
- Mayor Grumble's head no longer floats off during his entrance; only his hat pops up.
- Redesigned bosses take their new entry on update, and rewards and on/off settings are kept.
