# CBosses 1.3.4

## Changes

- Every boss now has one clean entrance: the whole model grows in at once, with a burst of particles in its theme's colours and its own roar. No more blocks flying in one by one, and no more rising out of the ground. Only the Rotting Giant still climbs out of the ground.
- The green sparkle particles are gone: nature bosses now drop cherry leaves and toxic bosses slime and smoke, and the shield spark is a cloud.
- The boss bar shows only the name and the health.
