# CBosses 1.3.2

## Changes

- Hoo, Thimble and Puffball are their own mini bosses now.
- The Antler King, Vorthax the Ender Wyrm and the Gale Sovereign have been removed. The finale now wakes after the Withered Tyrant and the Listener. Servers with an older config skip the removed bosses on their own.
- Includes 1.3.1: the old minis are back, the new big bosses use clean colours, Sir Clucks-a-Lot is removed, and Mayor Grumble's head no longer floats.
