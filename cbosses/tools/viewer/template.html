CQuests World Bosses

All 24 bosses as they are built in the game: block models with their own attacks, entrance and death. Drag to turn the camera, scroll or pinch to zoom. Weapons are gripped in the fist, elbows bend, and every boss has hit zones: a head hit, a weak spot and armour all change the damage. The browser draws rough stand-ins for the block textures; in game every piece uses the real Minecraft block.
