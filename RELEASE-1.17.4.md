# CQuests 1.17.4 and CBosses 1.3.14: flat skins on the older bosses

## Flat skins
- Stone, andesite, cobblestone, moss, sculk, wart and nether brick skins are now the flat block of the same colour on every model. Wool of every colour but white and black is concrete. 1,646 pieces changed or removed.

## Leftover patches
- Thirteen older bosses still had thin one-sided patches in colours that had nothing to do with them. Any thin patch with no twin on the other side of the body is gone: Abyssal Eye, Tide Tyrant, Mossback, the Old Wizard, the Horned Brute, the Rotting Giant, the Grave Marshal, Old Ironroot, the Frost Yeti, the Bog Orc, the Withered Tyrant, Brain Freeze and the Moss Spirit.

## Abyssal Eye
- The lure stalk was three sticks that did not touch. It is one bent rod now.
