# CQuests 1.17.8 and CBosses 1.3.18: every boss arrives and dies its own way

- Every one of the 93 bosses has its own entrance and its own death. No two are the same. The Lava Golem arrives as a burning meteor and grows out of the crater; the Yeti rises between ice spikes and freezes solid before it breaks; the Gravelord is pulled upright from under grave markers.
- Entrances and deaths now use loose blocks, not only particles: meteors that fall in, spikes that push out of the floor, a ring of blocks that closes in, rubble that rolls out, debris that rains down.
- Not every boss falls over when it dies. Many stay on their feet and burn, freeze, melt, implode, turn to stone, drift upward or burst.
- Every hit knocks two chips of the boss's own blocks loose.
- No model can sink into the ground any more, in any pose: the lowest block is measured every frame and the model is lifted when it dips under.
- The four hollows hold their staffs: the shaft runs through the fist.
- The Mossy Turtle and Mossback have real shells with raised plates. Planks are gone from every model (156 pieces).
- The Old Wizard has a gold hem, cuffs, belt, stars, brows and a moustache.
- Clucksworth's egg cracks three times before it breaks open.
- The gingerbread and egg decorations are iced and painted.
- Rideable Mossback (/mossback spawn): it now walks on any solid ground, steps up and down one block, and turns to where its rider looks. Before, it only moved on plain dirt.
- Loot crates: sneak and click a crate to see what it can hold and the chance of each item.
