# CQuests 1.8.1

Chapters cost a lot more to start. Just replace the jar.

| Chapter | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cost | 25k | 50k | 100k | 250k | 500k | 750k | 1M | 1.5M | 2.5M |

If your `config.yml` already has a `chapters.cost` section (it does once you edit it), your own numbers win: change them there and `/cq reload`. Everything else is the same as 1.8.0 (see RELEASE-1.8.0.md).

Tested on Paper 1.21.4, the newest Paper and the newest Folia. The JAR attached to the release is the exact file built and tested in that run.
