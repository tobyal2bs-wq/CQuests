# CQuests 1.17.1 and CBosses 1.3.11: everything on a boss moves

## Hands that hold
- Granny Morwen's staff, Mister Bones' shovel, Bjorn's rod and Grimsby's key now sit inside a closed fist and stay there through every move. Staffs stand on the ground.

## Everything moves (the eleven rebuilt bosses)
- Every empty hand has fingers and a thumb that curl while idle and clench on a hit.
- Jaws open on attacks and roars. Grimsby has a jaw with fangs now.
- Tongues, ears, noses, beards, hat tips, tails, coat tails, loincloths, Hedgey's beak and backpack, Sizzle's brows, bottles and sausage fork, Granny's cat and the flame on her staff all move on their own in every animation.
- Thimble, the little gnome brother, swings his own arms and legs.

## Hit boxes
- Each part of a rebuilt boss has its own hit box that follows it: head, body, hips, each upper arm and forearm, each thigh and shin, tail, wings, hat and the held weapon. Legs count 0.7, arms 0.8, the head 1.5, a weapon 0.3.
