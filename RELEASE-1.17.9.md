# CQuests 1.17.9 and CBosses 1.3.19: the serpent slithers, no boss shares a move

- Removed three bosses: Mama Gulp, Big Gus and Luna. They leave the rotation and the server's boss files on update.
- The Thorn Serpent uncoils when it moves: it lies out flat like a real snake and a wave runs down its body. It coils up again when it stops. It has a new slither rush at the player, and it calls four small serpents, small copies of itself.
- No two bosses share an attack or a hit reaction any more. All 399 clips are now each boss's own: its own wind-up timing, reach, twist into the blow and follow-through.
- Smashes bend the body: 63 slams, quakes and stomps that kept a straight back now lean back, fold over the blow and bend the knees.
- Mossback's shell plates are moss green, not brick.
