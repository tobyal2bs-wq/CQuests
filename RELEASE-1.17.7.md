# CQuests 1.17.7 and CBosses 1.3.17: parts that never moved now move

- Ten bosses had parts that stayed stiff in every animation. They move in idle, walk and every attack now.
- The Killer Bunny's ears flop and its basket swings. The Listener's tendrils wave. The Withered Tyrant's tail swings.
- The little gnome's beard, Killer Claus's sack and Clucksworth's shell rock. The Millstone Monk's jaw works.
- The Grand Augur's gem and the Prism Sentinel's ring turn. The Thorncoil Basilisk's coils shift against each other.
