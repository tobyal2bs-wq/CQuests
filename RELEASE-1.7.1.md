# CQuests 1.7.1

Better looking Discord posts. Everything else is the same as 1.7.0; just replace the jar.

* Every post is an embed with its own colour, title and emoji per event (chapter complete, boss defeated, daily streak, journey complete).
* The player's face is shown next to their name and their skin on the right (from mc-heads.net; change or turn off with `discord.player-head-url` and `discord.player-body-url`).
* Fields for the chapter and the next chapter, or the boss, the streak or the days on the road, plus the player's level and a story progress bar (▰▰▰▰▰▰▱▱▱▱ 60%).
* A footer with your server name, the webhook picture and a timestamp.
* New `discord.banner-url`: an optional wide picture under every post.

All 1.7.0 notes (chapters 6 to 10, private bosses, daily streaks, world scatter, placeholders) are in RELEASE-1.7.0.md.

Tested on Paper 1.21.4, the newest Paper and the newest Folia. The JAR attached to the release is the exact file built and tested in that run.
